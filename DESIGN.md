# Osoud Najd — Digital Design System / Stitch Brief

**Brand:** Osoud Najd General Contracting Establishment (أسود نجد للمقاولات العامة). Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Established 2022.

**Goal:** Present the establishment as a mature, design-conscious, dependable Saudi construction and execution partner for developers, project owners, procurement heads and main contractors — without inflating company size or making unsupported claims.

## Positioning
- Corporate architectural luxury. Editorial, restrained, premium and credible.
- Real project photography. Never fabricate or misattribute client-owned infrastructure to Osoud Najd; show only the execution scopes from the source company portfolio.
- Primary audience: Saudi project and procurement professionals.
- Bilingual: Arabic (RTL by default) + English (LTR).
- Avoid stock skyscrapers, fake statistics, made-up awards, generic hard-hat stock photos, excessive glassmorphism or gold gradients.

## Colors
- Architectural graphite: `#152023` (main text, dark fields)
- Midnight graphite: `#0F181B` (premium hero and sectional backgrounds)
- Stone white: `#F7F5F0` (base canvas)
- Warm stone: `#EEEAE2` (quiet supporting surfaces)
- Muted architectural bronze: `#B28A61` (small navigational and editorial highlights)
- Bronze light: `#D4B18B` (on dark backgrounds)
- Thin dividers: `#DCD8CF`
- Neutral text: `#687173`

## Typography
- Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic, weights 400, 500, 600, 700.
- English: DM Sans, 400, 500, 600, 700.
- Oversized editorial headlines with 1.12–1.25 line height; extremely readable on mobile.
- Do not use italic Arabic glyphs; emphasize second headline segment with a copper color.
- Labels in small tracked all-cap English, balanced with readable Arabic microcopy.

## Layout
- Maximum centered width 1380px, generous 22–78px side gutters.
- Editorial grid; not generic equal-sized cards. Selective wide project imagery with calm quiet spacing.
- Page hero: real construction photo, deep graphite overlay, large statement in the right-hand text column for RTL.
- Visible credibility row: founding date 2022, five disciplines, six curated projects (descriptive counts from the website, not contract values).
- Projects need case studies, not a grid of decorative photos; reference real source file scopes.
- Desktop 1440; tablet 768; mobile 390. Mobile navigation and typography designed first-class, not scaled down.

## Site architecture
1. Home: architectural hero, introduction, credibility row, selected projects, capabilities, method, contact CTA.
2. About: company background and grounded values.
3. Capabilities: five execution areas (concrete, steel, fit-out, roads/site, landscaping).
4. Projects: six curated case studies with filters.
5. Project detail template: source photography, factual scope, location and provenance.
6. Contact: clearly marked phone/email/map, mailto inquiry form and WhatsApp.

## Functional requirements
- Sticky refined header; bilingual language switch persists between pages.
- Semantic HTML, proper page titles, accessible navigation and skip link.
- Optimized local real photos with width/height, alt text, lazy-loading below the fold.
- Static hosting on Render; zero backend or invented lead capture.
- Phone `+966 54 905 9746`, office email `info@osoudnajd.com` (verify before final distribution).

> Design a **production-quality**, Arabic-first luxury corporate construction website for Osoud Najd General Contracting Est. Build an editorial architectural experience rather than an ordinary contracting template. Use the DESIGN.md tokens and guidelines provided. The first viewport should feel like a premium architecture journal: cinematic real on-site construction photograph, strong dark graphite contrast, precise bronze accent, giant controlled typography and refined CTA. Use real site images only. Include responsive desktop and mobile design, 6-page navigation, a 2-column asymmetric selected projects grid, detailed case-study pages, authentic capability showcase, and business inquiry CTAs. Avoid fabricated credentials or projects. Make all Arabic text natural, correctly right-aligned and well typeset. Use a premium, restrained motion language, excellent contrast and accessibility.
